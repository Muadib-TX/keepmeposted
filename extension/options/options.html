Analysis settings

Gemini analysis runs directly from this extension. No app server is required.

Stored in this browser profile. Do not use a key you cannot afford to expose from a local extension.

When a key is set, the article title, publication date, last update date, domain, and up to 10,000 characters of article text are sent to Google for analysis. The canonical URL is not sent. Without a key, analysis stays local and uses simpler heuristics.
